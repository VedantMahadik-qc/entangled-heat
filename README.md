## The brief in one paragraph

Build a **single-player GTA V (PC)** mod that is **free to download**, stays out of GTA Online, and **uses at least one Atlas quantum engine** through the Moth API. The quantum engine must **meaningfully affect the game**: "not just a filter or a hidden feature". Submit a **video** of it running, a **download link**, and **a few lines on how it uses Atlas**.
