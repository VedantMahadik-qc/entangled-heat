# Rules (summarised from the official terms, 28 Oct 2026)

Source: https://mothquantum.com/bounties/gtavmod/terms. Break any of these and you don't get paid.

## Who can enter
- 18 or over. Solo **or as a team** (team prizes go to the team lead).
- Free to enter.
- Excluded: Moth staff, contractors, advisors, judges, other Moth representatives, and their families.
- No entries from, or payments to, anyone on a UK/US sanctions list or living in a comprehensively sanctioned place (e.g. Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Cuba, occupied regions of Ukraine).

## What the mod must be
- [ ] Runs in **GTA V single-player on PC**
- [ ] Uses **at least one Atlas engine** (Moth may **check your Atlas account** to confirm the engines were really called)
- [ ] **Free to download**
- [ ] **Nothing in GTA Online**
- [ ] **Your own work**
- [ ] The quantum engine **meaningfully affects the game**, not just a filter or a hidden feature (from the brief)

## Content restrictions
- No leaked GTA VI material
- No Rockstar logos (this includes the thumbnail and the video)
- No brands, music or characters you don't have the rights to. **Use royalty-free or original music in the video.**

## How to enter
1. Make a free Atlas account and get an API key. **Don't leak the key in the code.**
2. Build the mod.
3. Record footage of it running.
4. Submit the video, a download link and a few lines on how it uses Atlas through the form by **9 Nov 2026, 23:59 UK**.
- More than one mod is allowed, but only one prize per person.

## Rights and data
- You keep the rights to your mod. Moth can show it, with credit, to promote the bounty and Atlas.
- Entry data is kept for 12 months. An email given for the brief is used only for this bounty unless you opt in.

## Prizes and payment
- 1st US$600 · 2nd US$300 · 3rd US$100, by bank transfer to your primary account in your tax-resident country (or a gift card if a transfer isn't possible).
- Prizes can't be exchanged. Winners pay their own taxes and fees.
- Age, ID and sanctions checks happen before payment. No reply within 14 days means the prize goes to the next entry.
- Judges' decisions are final. English law applies.
