# More ideas

The current pick is **Entangled Heat** (see STRATEGY.md). Everything below is a backup idea or an add-on.

## Extra submissions (you can enter several mods but only win one prize)

Rules for extra entries: each uses a **different main engine** so the judges don't think "seen this already", and each reuses `EntangledHeat.Core` (Atlas client, shot decks, offline cache) to keep the cost down.

| Pick | Mod | Main engine | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐ #2 | **Schrödinger's Maze**: a container maze on the airport runway, built from `labyrinth-v1` output. Walls are **in superposition**: every 30 s the maze shifts to another *measured* layout, so a route can close in front of you. Time trials on foot, by bike or by car; "IBM run" mazes measured on real hardware | Labyrinth | The engine was built for games, prop spawning is simple, the video is easy to read, and shifting walls are original |
| #3 | **Observer Effect**: pedestrians and traffic you're not looking at turn see-through "ghosts". When the camera looks at one, a real shot decides what it collapses into (civilian, cop, nothing, an unusual event). Look away and it goes back to superposition | Graph | A very clear "quantum" gameplay idea that's new for GTA |
| alt | **Quantum Chaos**: a chaos-mod-style effect every 30 s, picked by measurement, with **entangled effect pairs** that always fire together. The finale runs on IBM | Graph | Quick to build, but Chaos Mod V is famous, so it scores lower on originality |
| alt | **Quantum Surveillance**: witnesses' CCTV of you is a Quantum Blur image of your character. Blur strength depends on how you escaped, and it decides whether police recognise you later (the image shows on an in-game phone or "wanted poster") | Blur (+ Teleblur for disguises) | Uses the image engines in gameplay, not as a filter, but it's harder to make feel like core gameplay |

## Original concepts

| # | Idea | Engines | Quantum mechanic | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Observer Effect Traffic.** Traffic and pedestrians outside the camera's view exist as faded "ghost" possibilities. When you look at them they **collapse** into one real car or person. Turn away and they go back into superposition | Drive Engine | Measurement on observation | Medium |
| 2 | **Entangled Protagonists.** Michael, Franklin and Trevor are three entangled qubits. Switching characters is a *measurement*: one character's wanted level, cash or health is correlated with the others' | Drive Engine | Entanglement + collapse on switch | Medium |
| 3 | **Quantum Tunnelling.** Hold a key to tunnel through a wall, a car or the ground. Success and the exit point are sampled from the engine, so sometimes you appear inside a building. Teleblur morphs the screen mid-tunnel | Drive Engine + Teleblur | Tunnelling probability | Medium |
| 4 | **Schrödinger's Heist.** Guards and the vault sit in superposed positions (ghost markers) until you scan the area with the phone camera, which collapses them. Every run is a different heist | Labyrinth + Drive Engine | Superposition + phone "measurement" | High |
| 5 | **Entangled Getaway Cars.** Two linked cars: damage, speed boosts or a breakdown in one shows up in its partner across the map. Leave one as a decoy | Drive Engine | Bell-pair correlation | Low |
| 6 | **Quantum Street Races.** Labyrinth turns real Los Santos roads into a fresh checkpoint route each race. The finish line stays in superposition between two spots until the final lap | Labyrinth | Quantum walk routes | Low–Medium |
| 7 | **Uncertain Garage.** Each time you enter a car, Quantum Blur warps its handling data (grip, mass, torque). Teleblur blends its engine sound with a second car's | Blur + Teleblur | Blurred 3D/number data | Low |
| 8 | **Quantum Zombie Night.** Horde spawn points and waves come from a quantum walk. Infected NPCs are entangled, so killing one weakens or angers its partner | Labyrinth + Drive Engine | Walk + entanglement | Medium |

**Easiest to combine with Entangled Heat:** #1 (Observer Effect) and #5 (Entangled Getaway Cars). Both fit a police-chase mod and add an engine call without much extra scope.
